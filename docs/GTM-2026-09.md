## 1. ICP, jobs to be done, trigger events

### ICP A — HR Director / VP People / CHRO, 50-500 employees (self-serve buyer)
- Profile: 1-4 person HR team, Gusto or BambooHR or QuickBooks Payroll, Slack-heavy company, employees in 2-6 states, no in-house counsel. Series A-C tech, healthcare practices, professional services, light manufacturing.
- Jobs: stop being the human search engine for PTO/benefits/policy questions; answer consistently; never be the person who "told an employee the wrong thing" on ADA/FMLA; show the CFO a number.
- Triggers: first out-of-state hire; a handbook rewrite; an EEOC charge or attorney letter; a new HR hire replacing a departed one (knowledge gap); Series B/C growth spurt; an annual open enrollment wave of questions.

### ICP B — HR ops lead / COO inside a PE portfolio company, 200-2,000 employees (pilot then portfolio buyer)
- Profile: post-carve-out or roll-up, multiple business units or acquired brands with different handbooks, Rippling/ADP/Paylocity/UKG, an operating partner who reads the compliance line in every board pack.
- Jobs: consistent answers across BUs; a defensible record for diligence and exit; headcount avoidance in HR ops; sensitive-topic auditability the GC will accept.
- Triggers: add-on acquisition (handbook merge); a lawsuit at a sibling portco; operating-partner "AI in the back office" mandate; pre-exit diligence prep; HR leader turnover.

### Not ICP
Under 50 employees (no handbook, no budget), 5,000+ (Moveworks/Leena territory, procurement will demand SOC 2 we do not have yet), non-US.

## 2. Positioning and messaging hierarchy

One sentence: **AI HR Pilot is the AI HR policy and compliance copilot for HR teams of 50-2,000 that answers employees from the company's own handbook with citations, routes the risky questions to a human, and keeps the record — built by a 3x CHRO with a JD.**

Pillars (in order, and the order the homepage uses):
1. **Cited answers from your handbook** — not generic AI. The section is named every time.
2. **Risk caught before it is a claim** — scored escalation of ADA, FMLA, harassment, wage; federal + 11-state context; regulatory feed with sources.
3. **The record exists before anyone asks** — audit log, cases, roles, read-only payroll sync so paycheck questions get cited answers.

Proof we can say today (all verifiable in repo): federal + 11 states in the law database; 18 risk-weighted policy topics; 4 live read-only payroll/HRIS syncs; server-enforced roles and tenancy; founder credentials. Proof we cannot say yet: customer logos, testimonials, SOC 2, Slack/Teams. The site says so plainly; keep it that way until earned.

Message by audience:
- HR Director: "Stop being the search engine. Never be the one who told an employee the wrong thing."
- PE operator: "Consistent answers across every portco, and a record you can hand to diligence."
- CFO/GC (the approver): "Less than one coordinator's week per month; the compliance record is the part you cannot price until you need it."

## 6. Growth loops and retention
- **Escalation → policy gap → content loop:** the analytics "ghost policies" list (questions no document answers) becomes both the customer's to-do list and the founder's LinkedIn content calendar (anonymized, aggregated).
- **Regulatory feed → newsletter → site:** every regulatory update in the product becomes a Leverage Brief item linking to the relevant coverage row.
- **Portfolio loop:** one portco pilot → portfolio rate → sibling companies; each new portco adds handbook variety that improves escalation rules for all.
- **Retention levers:** day-7 ROI number in the dashboard (Tier 1 analytics), monthly PDF board pack for the HR lead to forward upward, quarterly policy review on Enterprise, and the founding-price lock.

## 9. Risks and mitigations
- **Procurement asks for SOC 2:** answer with the security page, the provider certifications, and a Type I timeline; target sub-500 buyers who accept that until it lands.
- **A wrong answer reaches an employee:** citations + confidence + human review on high risk are the product's mitigation; keep the incident playbook (`/security` contact) and log everything.
- **Founder bandwidth:** every founding-customer touch is also content; cap onboarding at 2 orgs/week until the onboarding checklist replaces the calls.
- **Competitor price drop (Workativ):** compete on triage + jurisdiction context + record, not price; never discount below Starter.
- **Clerk/Vercel config drift (the June outage):** Marketplace-provisioned keys, Sentry alerts on auth failures, `/api/health` monitored.
- **Claims drift on the site:** `tests/marketing-site.test.js` fails the build if pricing, coverage counts, public routes or the SOC 2 language drift.
